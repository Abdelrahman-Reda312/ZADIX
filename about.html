Our story

From one port to an entire coastline.

Zadix Services became one of the leading companies in the maritime industry by doing the fundamentals exceptionally well. Our company is based in Egypt, at the crossroads of the world's busiest shipping lane, and today we cover every Egyptian port and the full length of the Suez Canal.

As our clients' trust grew, so did our horizon. We have extended our activities to ports in Greece, Bulgaria, Romania and Turkey through carefully chosen, trusted partners who share our standards — giving owners and managers one reliable supply partner across the Eastern Mediterranean and the Black Sea.

Behind every delivery is a highly professional team that understands what clients really need: genuine product quality, suitable prices and absolute respect for time.

Our course

How Zadix set sail.

Chapter One

Founded in Egypt

Zadix begins supplying vessels in Egyptian ports, building its reputation one satisfied master at a time.

Chapter Two

The Suez Canal

We expand to serve ships in transit through the Canal and at Suez, Port Said, Adabiya and Sokhna — where speed is everything.

Chapter Three

Every Egyptian Port

From Alexandria and Damietta to Ras Gharib, Hurghada, Safaga, Abu Tartur and Hamrawein — complete national coverage.

Chapter Four

Beyond Our Borders

Through trusted partners, Zadix now serves vessels in Greece, Bulgaria, Romania and Turkey with the same standards.
