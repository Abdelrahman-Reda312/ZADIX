What we supply

Six disciplines. One seamless service.

Whatever your requisition contains, our team sources, inspects, packs and delivers it with the same care — so you can focus on the voyage ahead.

01
Provisions

Fresh, Frozen & Dry Provisions

Good food is the heartbeat of a happy crew. We supply fresh fruit and vegetables, meat, poultry and seafood, dairy, bakery, beverages and a full range of dry goods — sourced daily from trusted Egyptian producers and delivered in temperature-controlled conditions.

Fruit & VegetablesMeat & PoultrySeafoodDairy & EggsDry GoodsBeverages & Water
02
Deck Stores

Deck Stores & Marine Paints

From mooring lines to marine coatings, we keep your deck in working order. We source ropes, wires, shackles, tools, hardware and paints to your exact specifications so maintenance never stops.

Mooring RopesWires & ShacklesMarine PaintsHand & Power ToolsHardwareCleaning Chemicals
03
Engine Stores

Engine Stores & Technical Supplies

Your engineers depend on the right part at the right time. We supply lubricants, filters, gaskets, valves, pipes and fittings, electrical items and welding consumables to keep machinery running smoothly.

Lubricants & OilsFiltersValves & FittingsElectricalWelding SuppliesMeasuring Tools
04
Safety

Safety, Fire-Fighting & Medical

Safety is never optional. We provide life-saving appliances, fire-fighting equipment, personal protective equipment and medical stores to help your vessel remain compliant and your crew protected.

Life Buoys & JacketsFire ExtinguishersPPEFirst-Aid & MedicalSignals & FlaresSafety Signs
05
Cabin & Bonded

Cabin, Galley & Bonded Stores

Comfort aboard matters on long voyages. We supply bedding and linen, galley utensils, toiletries, cleaning materials, stationery and bonded stores — packed neatly and delivered ready for use.

Bedding & LinenGalley EquipmentToiletriesStationeryLaundry SuppliesBonded Stores
06
Logistics

Spare Parts Logistics & Delivery

When owners ship spare parts to Egypt, we take care of the rest: receiving, storing, coordinating clearance with your agent and delivering safely alongside — at berth, at anchorage or during Canal transit.

WarehousingClearance CoordinationLaunch DeliveryTruck DeliveryCanal Transit DeliveryCourier Handling
