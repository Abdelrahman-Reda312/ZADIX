Who we are

A name trusted on every quay of Egypt.

Zadix Services has grown into one of the leading companies in the maritime supply field. Built in Egypt and shaped by the rhythm of the Suez Canal, we understand that a ship's time in port is measured in hours — and that every hour matters.

Our highly professional team listens closely to each client's needs and answers them with three promises we never compromise: the quality of our products, prices that make sense, and deliveries that respect your schedule.

  • Full coverage of all Egyptian ports and the Suez Canal
  • A branch office in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates
  • Round-the-clock response, every day of the year
