Questions

Frequently asked.

Which ports do you cover?

We cover all Egyptian ports and the Suez Canal — Suez, Port Said, the Canal itself, Adabiya, Sokhna, Suez Oil Berth, Ras Gharib, Hurghada, Safaga, Abu Tartur, Hamrawein, Damietta and Alexandria — plus Greece, Bulgaria, Romania and Turkey through trusted partners.

How quickly can you deliver?

Delivery time depends on the port and the order, but we plan every delivery around your ETA and berth or anchorage window. Send us your schedule and we'll confirm a delivery plan with your quotation.

Can you supply ships transiting the Suez Canal?

Yes. We coordinate with your agent to deliver at the Canal anchorages or during transit, so your vessel can be supplied without changing its schedule.

What information should I send for a quote?

Your vessel name, port of call, ETA and requisition list are all we need. Add any brand or specification preferences and we'll match them.

Do you handle owners' spare parts?

Yes. We receive and store spare parts sent to Egypt, coordinate clearance with your agent and deliver them safely alongside.
